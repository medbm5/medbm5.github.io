Cyber risk · Monte Carlo

OpenCRQ

A cyber risk quantification platform (FAIR). A vectorised NumPy Monte Carlo engine runs 100,000 simulated years × 4 scenarios in ~489 ms, reporting expected annual loss, VaR95/99 and loss exceedance curves. A Bayesian network built from MITRE ATT&CK, and every number traceable to its source data.

  • FastAPI
  • NumPy
  • pgmpy
  • React
